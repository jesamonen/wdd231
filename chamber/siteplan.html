Site Plan: Osuwake Paints & Screeding Solutions

Individual Project Planning Document | WDD 231

Site Name

Proposed Name: Osuwake Paints & Screeding Solutions

Reasoning: This name carries direct brand equity and clearly defines the core offerings: specialized chemical paint manufacturing, wall preparation (screeding), and professional surface painting services. It establishes instant authority for potential clients seeking structural finishing expertise.

Optional Domain Availability: osuwakepaints.com

Site Purpose

The website serves as a primary digital storefront for residential, commercial, and industrial clients. It provides comprehensive details on custom chemical paint formulations (matte, satin, emulsions), professional wall screeding applications, transparent pricing estimates, and visual proofs of past surface-finishing projects alongside easy contact pathways.

Target Audience Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: "What is the difference between wall screeding and standard painting, and how much will it cost to prep and finish my 3-bedroom building?"
  • Scenario 2: "Does Osuwake Paints manufacture custom paint colors directly, and how can I request a sample or custom color mixing for a commercial project?"
  • Scenario 3: "Where can I view real photos of completed residential wall screeding projects and contact the team directly for an on-site evaluation?"

Color Scheme

The selected palette balances professional corporate structure with vibrant creative accents, reflecting precision chemical formulation and high-end decorative finishing. All text combinations strictly meet WCAG AAA contrast guidelines.

Primary: #1B365D
(Deep Navy Blue)
Secondary: #D97706
(Warm Amber / Gold)
Accent: #F1F5F9
(Light Slate / Mist)

Color Application Rules

  • Primary Color (#1B365D): Main header backgrounds, primary headings (<h1>, <h2>), navigation bar, and footer base.
  • Secondary Color (#D97706): Call-to-action buttons, pricing highlights, active navigation links, and subheadings (<h3>).
  • Accent Color (#F1F5F9): Card section backgrounds, table headers, form field containers, and soft structural dividers.
  • Text Color (#1E293B): Dark charcoal typography across all light sections to ensure crisp contrast and effortless reading.

Typography

Two complementary Google Fonts are used to separate structural headers from descriptive text content.

1. Primary Heading Font: Montserrat

Montserrat — Premium Interior & Exterior Paint Finishes

Usage: Page headers, section headings (<h1>, <h2>, <h3>), hero banner titles, and navigation links.

2. Body Typography: Open Sans

Open Sans — Our custom chemical paint formulations deliver superior opacity, weather protection, and smooth matte or satin wall finishes for long-lasting coverage.

Usage: Paragraph text, service feature bullet points, contact form fields, testimonial quotes, and footer information.

Wireframes

Blueprint sketches detailing the responsive layout structure for the Home page across mobile and desktop viewports.

Small Viewport (Mobile)

Single-column layout with a hamburger navigation menu, vertically stacked service cards, and full-width CTA buttons.

Large Viewport (Desktop)

Horizontal header navigation, multi-column grid layout for core services (Paint Production, Screeding, Painting), and split gallery/contact grid.
